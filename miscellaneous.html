Stage 1 & Other

Miscellaneous Simulations

Simulations that span multiple topics or belong to Stage 1 content.

Waves

Standing Waves
Ruben's Tube Watch standing waves form in a gas column as flame height traces the pressure nodes and antinodes.

Total Internal Reflection
Fibre Optic Cable Light bounces along a flexible glass fibre by total internal reflection. Drag handles to reshape the cable, change n and λ, and watch light leak out where the bend is too tight.

Spatial Beats
Moiré Effect Stack two periodic patterns — lines, dots, circles, radial, triangle or hex — and watch large fringes appear. A tiny rotation or spacing mismatch becomes the spatial analogue of audio beats.

Biology

Natural Selection
Natural Selection with Frogs Simulate predation and reproduction over multiple generations; track allele frequencies with a live graph. Natural Selection with Frogs (no graph) Same simulation with the graph removed — students record data manually in a table.

Mathematics

Cryptography
Classroom Ciphers One message box, eight pen-and-paper ciphers: Caesar shift (and ROT13), keyword, Vigenère, Playfair, Polybius square (and tap code), scytale, Morse code and pigpen. Encode or decode, flip the answer back into the box, and see the working a student would write: the shifted alphabet, every Caesar shift at once, the keyword alphabet with the letters it leaves unchanged, the keyword under the message with the Vigenère square, the Playfair square and its pairs, letters as row-and-column coordinates in the Polybius grid, the scytale's rod and unwound strip, a Morse chart with playback, and the pigpen grids with a tap-to-decode keypad. Enigma Machine A working Enigma I, M3 or M4. Set the rotors, ring settings, reflector and plugboard from a real key sheet, then type on your own keyboard: the rotors turn (double step included), a lamp lights, and the paper tape records the message. A wiring diagram follows the current from key to reflector and back to the lamp, showing why no letter can encrypt to itself. Includes a real 1941 message, and three messages to decode by setting the machine from a printed key sheet.

Energy

Kinetic & Gravitational Potential Energy
Rollercoaster Builder Build a coaster piece by piece in an isometric park, then send a train round it and watch the energy move. Chain lift, drops, banked or flat turns, loops and brake runs; live kinetic, potential and thermal bars against the energy actually supplied, energy and g-force plotted along the track, and a ride report with vertical and lateral g. A path-independence demo races three trains down three different routes from the same height. Ep = mgh, Ek = ½mv².
